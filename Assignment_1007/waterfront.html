Mountains Meet the City

Vancouver's waterfront offers some of the clearest examples of the city's connection with its natural surroundings.

Visitors can see the harbour, boats, downtown buildings, and mountains while walking through the same area.

Why I Recommend It

The waterfront is especially enjoyable when you want a simple walk with great scenery. It is also easy to combine with other downtown attractions.

  • Walk beside Vancouver Harbour.
  • Look across the water toward the mountains.
  • Enjoy the city skyline from a different angle.

Vancouver Waterfront

Vancouver's waterfront is one of the best places to experience the scenery that makes the city special.

Visitors can see the harbour, mountains, boats, and modern buildings while walking through the waterfront area.

Why Visit?

  • Enjoy wide views of Vancouver Harbour and the mountains.
  • Walk along the waterfront close to downtown attractions.
  • Experience both natural scenery and the city skyline.

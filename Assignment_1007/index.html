Welcome to Vancouver

Vancouver is one of my favourite cities because urban life and natural scenery are always close together.

This website introduces four attractions that show different sides of Vancouver, from downtown streets to forests, markets, ocean views, and mountains.

Visitor Tips

  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking.
  • Bring an extra layer for changing weather.
  • Leave enough time to enjoy the scenery.

Why Vancouver?

A city where mountains and ocean are always close by.

Vancouver is especially interesting because visitors do not have to choose between city activities and outdoor scenery.

Each page in this website introduces a different part of that experience.

Welcome to Vancouver

Vancouver is a city where urban life and natural scenery are very close together. Visitors can explore the city, walk beside the ocean, enjoy large parks, and see mountain views during the same trip.

This website introduces four Vancouver attractions that I recommend visiting.

Downtown Vancouver

Downtown Vancouver combines shopping, restaurants, waterfront scenery, and views of the surrounding mountains.

Explore Downtown Vancouver

Stanley Park

Stanley Park combines forest, ocean views, walking paths, and beautiful scenery close to downtown Vancouver.

Explore Stanley Park

Granville Island

Granville Island is a relaxing place for food, local markets, small shops, and waterfront views.

Explore Granville Island

Vancouver Waterfront

The Vancouver waterfront is a great place to enjoy harbour, mountain, and city views at the same time.

Explore Vancouver Waterfront

Visitor Tips

A few simple tips can make exploring Vancouver easier:

  • Wear comfortable shoes because many areas are best explored on foot.
  • Bring an extra layer because the weather can change during the day.
  • Leave enough time to enjoy the scenery instead of rushing between places.

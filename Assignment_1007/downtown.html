Explore Downtown

Downtown Vancouver is the centre of the city's business, shopping, dining, and entertainment areas.

What makes downtown especially interesting is its location. Busy streets and modern buildings are surrounded by the harbour, mountains, and several waterfront areas.

Why I Recommend It

Downtown is a good place for a first visit because many attractions are close together and public transportation makes it easy to explore the rest of Vancouver.

  • Walk around the downtown streets.
  • Enjoy harbour and mountain views.
  • Explore restaurants and shopping areas.

Downtown Vancouver

Downtown Vancouver is one of the best places to begin exploring the city. It combines modern buildings, restaurants, shopping, public transportation, and waterfront scenery.

What I like most about downtown is the contrast between the busy city and the natural scenery surrounding it. The ocean and mountains are visible from many parts of the area.

Why Visit?

  • Enjoy city and mountain views in the same area.
  • Find many restaurants, shops, and attractions nearby.
  • Use public transportation to easily explore other areas.

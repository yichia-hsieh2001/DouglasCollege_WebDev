A Natural Escape

Stanley Park is one of Vancouver's best-known natural attractions and one of my favourite places in the city.

Visitors can experience large trees, walking paths, waterfront scenery, and views across the harbour while remaining very close to downtown.

Why I Recommend It

Stanley Park shows how closely Vancouver is connected to nature. It is a good destination when you want a quieter experience after spending time in the city.

  • Walk beside the ocean.
  • Enjoy forest scenery.
  • Look for views of the skyline and mountains.

Continue Exploring

After enjoying nature, visit Granville Island for food and local culture.

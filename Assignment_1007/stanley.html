Stanley Park

Stanley Park is one of Vancouver's most famous natural attractions. It offers forest scenery, ocean views, walking paths, and views of the city.

It is one of my favourite places in Vancouver because visitors can enjoy nature without travelling far from downtown.

Why Visit?

  • Walk through forests and along the waterfront.
  • Enjoy views of the ocean, mountains, and Vancouver skyline.
  • Take a relaxing break from the busy downtown area.

Visitor Voices

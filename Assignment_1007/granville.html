Granville Island

Granville Island is known for its public market, food, local businesses, shops, and creative atmosphere.

I recommend Granville Island for a slower day when you want to enjoy food, walk near the water, and explore local shops.

Why Visit?

  • Explore the famous public market and local food.
  • Walk around small shops and creative businesses.
  • Enjoy relaxing waterfront views around False Creek.

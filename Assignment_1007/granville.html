A Different Side of Vancouver

Granville Island is known for its public market, food, small shops, and creative atmosphere.

Compared with downtown, the area feels slower and more relaxed. Visitors can explore the market and then spend time walking beside False Creek.

Why I Recommend It

Granville Island is a good choice for visitors who want to combine food, shopping, and waterfront scenery in the same place.

  • Explore the public market.
  • Try local food and snacks.
  • Walk beside False Creek.
